CHIHARU SHIOTA The Soul Trembles

Chiharu Shiota’s immersive and monumental works draw you into a poetic world of emotion and memory.

Making its first North American stop at the MMFA, this exhibition is a rare opportunity to discover the singular work of this world-renowned Japanese artist.

Prepare to be moved!
