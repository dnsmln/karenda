Centaur Theatre Presents

KISSES DEEP

By Michel Marc Bouchard

Translated by Linda Gaboriau

Directed by Eda Holmes

With Lyndz Dantiste, Yves Jacques, Leni Parker, Alice Pascual and Kevin Raymond

In this long-awaited English-language premiere, Michel Marc Bouchard takes audiences on a thrilling, passionate journey of self-discovery and redemption. Originally premiering in French at TNM in a co-production with Centaur in 2020, Kisses Deep, translated into English by the acclaimed Linda Gaboriau, will be directed by Centaur’s Artistic Director Eda Holmes (who directed the original French production) and will run from November 26 to December 14, 2025.

At the centre of the story is Hugo, a young man in the banlieues of Montreal consumed by his passion for high fashion and dreams of becoming a designer. Inspired by his idol Yves Saint Laurent, Hugo channels his creativity and obsession into a singular mission: to restore the tarnished reputation of his mother, Béatrice, after she is arrested for assault. Determined to help her reclaim her dignity, Hugo decides to save his mother by designing the perfect outfit for her upcoming court appearance.

As Hugo works tirelessly to create his masterpiece, he is faced with the complexities of his relationship with Béatrice. The two are bound by a deep, often painful love, and Hugo’s artistic pursuit becomes a means of both confronting and healing the wounds of their past. Through this heartfelt and visually striking exploration, Kisses Deep delves into the essence of artistic creation—questioning whether art can serve as a source of comfort or merely a reflection of deep-seated pain.The production features Leni Parker as Béatrice and Kevin Raymond as Hugo, with Lyndz Dantiste, Yves Jacques, and Alice Pascual rounding out the cast of characters. Kisses Deep is a poignant, evocative work that explores the transformative power of art, blending emotional depth with an exploration of the world of fashion and design. This production promises to be an unforgettable experience, filled with both glamour and raw emotion.

*NEW THIS YEAR*

“All My Relations” Tickets

As part of our ongoing commitment to welcoming Indigenous community members at Centaur – both on-stage and off – this season we are excited to launch a new “All My Relations” ticket tier for all subscription-series Centaur productions. Inspired by our colleagues at NAC Indigenous Theatre, this special $15 ticket price is reserved for First Nations, Inuit, and Métis community members. It is a symbol of Centaur’s eagerness to open our doors to Indigenous audiences so that we can all gather to experience the magic of theatre together.

“All My Relations” tickets are available online, in-person, or by calling the box office.
