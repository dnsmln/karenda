About

The Cinéma Moderne is your neighborhood cinema in the heart of Montreal’s Mile-End. It is a unique, vibrant and welcoming cultural place, on a human scale. Accessible and varied, the in-theatre and online programming invites you to discover the most original works of Quebec, Canadian and international cinema. Short films and special events will also be in the spotlight to decompartmentalize the cinema and create happenings.
