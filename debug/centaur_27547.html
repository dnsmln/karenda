MORALITY BITES!

MORALITY BITES! is a series of short plays with a difference. On the menu? Moral dilemmas - with a side of humour. Come chew on thorny questions and tasty snacks with Theatre Ouest End.

Event Times

October 26, 2026 - 8:00 pm

Ticket Prices free

Sponsors

Share

After a successful run at the Centaur Theatre Gallery, Theatre Ouest End is thrilled to bring their popular series MORALITY BITES! back for the 2026-2027 season. MORALITY BITES! offers an evening of 2 short plays–each examining a different bite-size moral dilemma–with a side of humour. These are dramatic readings with a difference as the audience is invited to weigh in on the choices the characters make. Did they do the right thing? What is the right thing? Is there even a right thing to do?  Come join us to chew on some thorny questions and tasty snacks with Theatre Ouest End!

Dates: October 26, 2026 / December 14, 2026 / February 22, 2027 / April 12, 2027

Each event will take place at 7:30pm in the Centaur Theatre Gallery and admission is free of charge. All are welcome!
